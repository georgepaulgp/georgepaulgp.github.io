Projects & Research

Bayesian Nonparametric Quantile Function Modelling for Tail Risk Forecasting

Research proposal – Functional Gaussian Process approach to volatility timing

This project proposes a monotone functional Gaussian process quantile regression model that treats the entire conditional quantile function as a smooth surface over quantile level and predictors. The aim is to deliver calibrated joint VaR and ES forecasts with full uncertainty quantification, avoiding quantile crossing via a non‑negative latent process over the quantile index and constrained variational inference.

Empirically, the work evaluates S&P 500 daily returns from 1990–2025, comparing tail calibration, proper scoring rules, and volatility‑timing performance against GARCH, Bayesian parametric quantile regression, and machine‑learning baselines under realistic transaction costs.

Bayesian modelling for energy and emissions

Advancing Hierarchical Bayesian Neural Networks for Building Energy Use

MSc research project · Distinction (81%)

Hierarchical Bayesian models were developed for Energy‑Use Intensity prediction in office buildings, combining domain‑informed priors with flexible likelihoods and posterior predictive checks. The work emphasised uncertainty quantification for transparent carbon assessments and explored how model structure reflects building heterogeneity.

The resulting models produced accurate, interpretable EUI forecasts with quantified uncertainty, strengthening the foundation for audit‑aligned emissions reporting and decision‑support tools in commercial real estate.
